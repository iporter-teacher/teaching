Five Dimensions of Curiosity

What makes one person more curious than another? Rather than regard curiosity as a single trait, we can break it down into five distinct dimensions. Instead of asking, “How curious are you?” we can ask, “How are you curious?”

Scoring Instructions

Compute the average score (mean of the 5 items) for each dimension. Reverse-score the items under Stress Tolerance (1 = 7, 2 = 6, 3 = 5, 4 = 4, 5 = 3, 6 = 2, 7 = 1) so that higher scores reflect greater tolerance for stress and uncertainty. By comparing your results with those of a nationally representative sample of people in the United States, you can determine whether you are Low, Medium, or High on each dimension.
